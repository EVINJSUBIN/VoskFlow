How it works

Three steps. Zero friction.

Chapter 01

Press & Hold

Hold Ctrl+Space anywhere on your system. VoskFlow activates instantly.

Chapter 02

Speak Naturally

The Vosk engine captures your microphone stream and transcribes it in real-time. Fully offline. No latency.

Chapter 03

Release to Type

Let go of the key. Your words are instantly typed into the active window — VS Code, Discord, Chrome, anything.

Features

100% Offline & Private

Zero network calls. Zero telemetry. Your audio stream is processed frame-by-frame on-device and discarded immediately. Nothing is stored. Nothing is sent. Ever.
